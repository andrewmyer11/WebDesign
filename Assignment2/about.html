The Movie The Secret Life of Walter Mitty is a movie that really changes how you feel about the world and traveling. When it comes to this movie you will be sucked into what feels like a whole new world that you never expected to jump into. It truly makes my want to travel more then you already do to see the world and all it has to offer. The intro of this film makes you wonder what you are watching, but by the end of it you feel this warm feeling about traveling more and not taking life for granite.
